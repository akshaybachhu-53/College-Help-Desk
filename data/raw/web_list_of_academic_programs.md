# List of Academic Programmes — IIT Jodhpur (web page snapshot)

Source page: https://www.iitj.ac.in/office-of-academics/en/list-of-academic-programs
Captured: 25 October 2026. The page is a table; this file records its content in summarised form (programme names, counts and start years).

## Bachelor of Technology (B.Tech.) — 10 programmes
Computer Science and Engineering; Electrical Engineering; Mechanical Engineering; Bioengineering; Materials Engineering; Chemical Engineering; Civil and Infrastructure Engineering; Artificial Intelligence and Data Science; Electronics Engineering; Aerospace Engineering.
Computer Science and Engineering, Electrical Engineering and Mechanical Engineering started in 2008; Bioengineering started in 2017.

## Bachelor of Science (B.Sc. / B.S.) — 6 programmes
Chemistry; Physics; Mathematics and Computing; Management and Technology (blended mode); Engineering Chemistry; Engineering Physics.

## Integrated Teacher Education Programme (B.Sc.-B.Ed., ITEP)
One programme with a B.Sc. major in Physics, Chemistry or Mathematics, starting in AY 2024-25.

## Master of Science (M.Sc.) — 5 programmes
Chemistry, Mathematics and Physics (all from 2015); Digital Humanities; Computational Social Science.

## Master of Technology (M.Tech.) — 26 programmes
Areas include Artificial Intelligence, Cyber-Physical Systems, Communication Systems, Robotics, VLSI, Energy, Environmental Engineering, Composite and Battery Materials, and Power Electronics. M.Tech. in Automotive Engineering is scheduled to start in AY 2027-28.

## Executive M.Tech. — 5 programmes
Artificial Intelligence; Data and Computational Sciences; Intelligent VLSI Systems; Augmented and Virtual Reality; Robotics and Mobility Systems.

## Master of Design (M.Des.)
One programme, starting in 2025.

## Doctor of Philosophy (Ph.D.) — 20 programmes
Covers the science and engineering departments, Management and Entrepreneurship, AI and Data Science, the School of Design, the School of Liberal Arts and several research centres.

## Interdisciplinary Ph.D. — 5 programmes
Digital Humanities; Smart Healthcare; Space Science and Technology; Robotics and Mobility Systems; Quantum Information and Computation (all started in 2019).

## Other programmes
- MBA (School of Management and Entrepreneurship): started in AY 2020-21.
- Masters and Ph.D. Dual Degree, and Masters, in Medical Technologies: started in AY 2020-21.
- Post-graduate Diploma in Data Engineering and Cloud Computing: started in AY 2022-23.
