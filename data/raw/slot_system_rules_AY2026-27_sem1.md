## Notes and rules

- Monday 5:00-5:50 pm: Free Slot for Department Activities/ Tutorials
- Tuesday 5:00-5:50 pm: Professional Ethics - I (UG)/ Free Slot for Department Activities / Tutorials
- Wednesday 5:00-5:50 pm: Technical Communication (PG) /Tutorials
- Thursday 5:00-5:50 pm: System Engineering and Project Management (PG) / Tutorials
- L slots: For labs and Tutorial for courses in F-J / M slots: For labs and Tutorial for courses in A-E
- While allocation of slots for UG/PG compulsory courses, priority would be given to students of graduating btech with backlog 2 or less
- Maximum students per TUTORIAL ~40. Tutorial slots are flexible (any timing) as per the convenience of students and instructors and classroom availability. Some slots are provided in the time table above but any other timing can also be considered based on classroom availability.
- Maximum students per lab 50.
- All fractals of a course would run in the same slot.
- In order to facilitate electives/overload for 2nd and 3rd year students, all Departments will open atleast 1 core/elective courses in K or N slot.
- Upper limit of HSS course is 80, All elective courses is 80 and Managment Minor course is 40.
