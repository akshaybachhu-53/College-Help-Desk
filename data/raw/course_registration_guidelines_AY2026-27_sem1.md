# Course Registration Guidelines — AY 2026-27 Semester I (Office of Academics, ERP registration notice)

Source: "Important Points" section of the course-registration notice circulated by the Office of Academics with the AY 2026-27 Semester I course list. Copied from the notice text.

## Important points

1. Regular students are not allowed to register in Executive slots, and Executive students are not allowed to register in Regular slots.
2. Regular and Executive students can register for courses floated in hybrid slots (slots like O, P, Q, R, S).
3. Courses are to be registered slot-wise, and students will be allowed to register for only one course per slot.
4. Students with backlogs are advised to register for the Basic Courses and complete them before registering for the Department Compulsory Courses. In case of any confusion, please consult the Faculty Advisor.
5. Students must register for the appropriate non-graded courses. It is mandatory to complete the non-graded courses for the degree requirements.
6. For any course-related query (course type, credit requirements, etc.), discuss with your Faculty Advisor. Faculty Advisors also have the option to register courses, but only within the deadline.
7. The following course levels are applicable to all UG and PG students:

| S.N. | Level | Category | Credit requirement to register |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 100 | Courses for B.Tech. programmes | B.Tech. (Cr >= 0) |
| 2 | 200 | Courses for B.Tech. programmes | B.Tech. (Cr >= 0) |
| 3 | 300 | Courses for B.Tech. programmes | B.Tech. (Cr >= 38) |
| 4 | 400 | Courses for B.Tech. programmes | B.Tech. (Cr >= 75) |
| 5 | 500 | Preparatory courses of M.Sc. and M.Tech. programmes (NOT open to B.Tech. and B.Tech. + M.Tech. Dual Degree) | M.Tech. (Cr = 0), M.Sc. (Cr = 0) |
| 6 | 600 | Courses for M.Sc. programmes | B.Tech. (Cr >= 75), M.Sc. (Cr >= 0), M.Tech. (Cr >= 0) |
| 7 | 700 | Courses for M.Tech. programmes; advanced courses for M.Sc. | B.Tech. (Cr >= 85 with CGPA >= 6), M.Sc. (Cr >= 30), M.Tech. (Cr >= 0), Ph.D. (Cr >= 0) |
| 8 | 800 | Advanced courses for M.Tech. and Ph.D. programmes. An 800-level course should have a 700-level course as a prerequisite. | M.Tech. (Cr >= 14), Ph.D. (Cr >= 0) |

Note: the limit of credits used to check course level has been updated as follows:
"Limit of credits to check course level = credits earned by the student till the last semester + credits currently registered in the current semester."
a) This assumes that the student will pass all the courses. If there is a shortfall, the student will be dropped from the courses during the main registration.
b) Such students will be permitted to register for other courses during the main registration.

8. If you find any discrepancy in course type or course category, contact your departmental timetable committee member.
9. For Ph.D. students: refer to Circular No. IITJ/OAA/Circular/2024-25/5587, "Guidelines for submission of thesis and subsequent registration to the next semester for PhD students", for registration.
10. For Institute Humanities electives, the upper cap on the number of students is 80. Allocation will be based on the CGPA earned till the last semester.
11. For the Minor in Entrepreneurship and the Department Specialization in Engineering Innovation, a scrutiny committee will be formed to select students. The upper cap of students for Minor in Management courses is 40.
12. For pre-registration there is no upper cap on elective courses; however, an upper cap will be implemented during main registration. First preference will be given to students enrolled in the course in a category other than open elective.

For technical issues, write to ERP IIT Jodhpur; for any other registration issue, write to Registration IIT Jodhpur. Please use "reply" instead of "reply all".
