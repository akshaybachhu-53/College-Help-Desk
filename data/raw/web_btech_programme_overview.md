# Bachelor of Technology at IIT Jodhpur — programme overview (web page snapshot)

Source page: https://www.iitj.ac.in/Bachelor-of-Technology/en/Bachelor-of-Technology
Captured: 25 October 2026 (summarised; the links to the curriculum and programme-structure files on this page had no targets at capture time).

## Programmes listed on the page
Eight B.Tech. programmes: Bioscience and Bioengineering; Computer Science and Engineering; Electrical Engineering; Mechanical Engineering; Artificial Intelligence and Data Science; Chemical Engineering; Materials Engineering; Civil and Infrastructure Engineering.

## Curriculum revision (2019)
The institute revised its undergraduate curriculum in 2019 around outcome-based education, with the aim of preparing "global engineers" for challenges such as energy, food, water and sustainable development.

## Key features of the curriculum
- A strong grounding in basic and engineering sciences.
- Non-graded courses in social connect, performing arts and communication skills.
- Early design courses focused on conceptual design.
- Entrepreneurship offered as a minor.
- Five-month industry or R&D practical projects under Department Area Specialisation.
- A five-year B.Tech. + M.Tech. option.
- Interdisciplinary specialisations such as AI, Cyber-Physical Systems, IoT, Robotics and Smart Healthcare.
- A double B.Tech. with a second degree in Engineering Science.
- Continuous assessment alongside examinations, and finer-grained grading.

## Guiding principles stated on the page
All students can learn, though not at the same pace or in the same way; success should open doors to further success; institutions should create conditions for success; challenging initiatives should be encouraged without fear of failure.
